Cut & Fit

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile puzzle game Cut & Fit (the “Game”), published by Agara (“we”, “us”), handles information when you play it. The Game is available on Android (package com.agara.cutandfit).

Summary

  • We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your puzzle progress and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The Game shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect device and usage information to deliver and measure those ads, as described below.

Information stored on your device

The Game saves the following locally on your device so you can continue where you left off. This information is not sent to us or to any server operated by us.

  • Puzzle progress: current and unlocked levels, stars, best scores, best cut counts, times and hints used.
  • Settings: sound effects, ambient music, haptics and developer tools.
  • Generated puzzle data cached to make levels load faster.

You can delete this information at any time by clearing the Game’s storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the Game.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

When you choose to watch a video to receive a hint, the Game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob), provided by Google LLC. To serve, limit the frequency of, and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as:

DataPurpose
Device identifiers, including the Android advertising IDAd delivery, frequency capping, measurement and, where you have agreed, personalised ads
IP address and approximate location derived from itAd delivery and fraud prevention
Ad interactions and app usage (for example, ads viewed or tapped)Ad measurement and analytics
Diagnostics (for example, crash and performance data of the ads SDK)Keeping ads working reliably

This information is collected and processed by Google, not by us. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Ads are only shown when you choose to watch a video. Playing the Game does not require watching ads.

Sharing of information

We do not sell or share personal information. The only third party that receives data from the Game is Google AdMob, as described above.

Your choices and consent

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): where required by law, the Game asks for your consent through Google’s consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can review or change your choice at any time with Privacy choices in the Game’s Settings.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android device settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads).
  • Not watching ads: you can always decline the video and keep playing.

Permissions

The Game uses internet access to load ads, and the advertising ID permission used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, files or precise location.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us and we will help remove it.

Data security and retention

Data stored on your device stays there until you clear it or uninstall the Game. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google’s own policies linked above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game changes. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact
